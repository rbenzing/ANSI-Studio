# Code of Conduct

ANSI Studio aims to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and keep discussion focused on the work.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory, sexualized or demeaning language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [@rbenzing](https://github.com/rbenzing), through the contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may block contributors who do not follow this code.
